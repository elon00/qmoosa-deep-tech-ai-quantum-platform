The Midnight Solution

Midnight: Dual-Ledger Architecture & Compact Language

Dual-Ledger Separation

• Private State (Witness): Private balances, agent secret keys, and raw telemetry remain strictly local on the user's machine.
• Public State (Ledger): On-chain commitments, anti-replay nullifiers, and proof counters verified globally by the network.

Compact Smart Contract Language

• TypeScript-Based Simplicity: Compact abstracts complex ZK polynomial constraint generation into intuitive TypeScript-like syntax.
• Native Cryptographic Primitives: Built-in persistent_hash and transient_hash for binding commitments and selective disclosure.

Circuit 1

Zero-Knowledge Solvency Proof (prove_solvency_private)

Private Assertions

Asserts that private_balance ≥ min_solvency_threshold without publishing the balance.

Binding Commitment

Computes persistent_hash(private_balance, private_salt) matching the registered on-chain commitment.

Anti-Replay Nullifier

Generates nullifier from secret key & salt. Replaying the same nullifier is rejected fail-closed by the public ledger registry.

Circuit 2

Selective Disclosure (selective_disclose_compliance)

Privacy-Preserving Audit Token

Proves that an autonomous agent or workflow satisfied compliance (score ≥ 95/100) and issues a transient cryptographic token verifiable ONLY by authorized auditors.

Zero Telemetry Leakage

The auditor confirms the compliance certification hash, but raw PII, sensitive user prompts, and proprietary model weights remain 100% encrypted and unexposed.

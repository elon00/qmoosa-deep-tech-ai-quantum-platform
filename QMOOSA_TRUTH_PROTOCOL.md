# QMoosa Truth Protocol

## Evidence states

Use the narrowest status supported by evidence:

- **REPOSITORY_TESTED** — source/tests/CI support the stated repository behavior.
- **SIMULATION** — mathematical, local, mocked, synthetic or emulated behavior.
- **TESTNET_VERIFIED** — requires reproducible public testnet evidence for the exact deployment.
- **EXTERNALLY_VERIFIED** — requires an independent source/auditor/provider that can be re-queried.
- **PRODUCTION_VERIFIED** — requires deployment, security, operational and workload evidence; green CI alone is insufficient.
- **BLOCKED** — required evidence is absent or a safety boundary intentionally prevents use.

## Rules

- Never convert an internal score, badge, generated certificate or CI result into third-party certification.
- Never present illustrative clients/testimonials, synthetic telemetry or demo transactions as real customer/network evidence.
- Never publish latency, TPS, cost, accuracy, fidelity, availability or throughput as general performance without a documented workload/environment.
- Never claim a partnership, award, grant, mainnet deployment or customer unless externally verifiable evidence is linked.
- Standardized algorithms such as ML-KEM/ML-DSA do not make the application a FIPS-validated module.
- Quantum simulators, cellular automata and mathematical models are not physical QPU execution.
- Missing evidence is **NOT VERIFIED**, not PASS.
- Secrets and private keys must never be committed.

## URS

The Universal Reality System (URS) is a **repository-defined internal regression/evidence framework**. A 12/12 or 10.0/10 internal score means only that those internal checks passed. It is not an audit, certification, legal determination or production-readiness verdict.
